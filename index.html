Meet Jalynn Craig

“Business is ministry when surrendered to God; your ideas or talents are strategic for God’s great purpose for you and His Kingdom in heaven.”

Jalynn Craig is a faith-driven entrepreneur, marketplace minister, and real estate mentor who dares to be both controversial and Kingdom-focused. She founded Socialite Sells Academy where faith and business collide — not in theory, but in practice, in profit, and in purpose.

Through the Milk & Honey Club on Clubhouse, powered by Heir Productions, Jalynn leads a global ministry community — standing firmly on God's promises in Ephesians 1 — equipping believers for exponential breakthrough in the marketplace.
